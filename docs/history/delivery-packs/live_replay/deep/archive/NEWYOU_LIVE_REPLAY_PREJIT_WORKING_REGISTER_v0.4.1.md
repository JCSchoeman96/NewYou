# 4. Accepted LIVE-UPD-006 candidate

## LIVE-UPD-006 — Define post-capture recording-purpose withdrawal consequences

**Working status:** `ACCEPTED_WORKING_LOCK / CANDIDATE UPSTREAM DECISION PACKAGE / NOT AUTHORITY`

The accepted working direction is that existing OQ-021 / Product / Privacy / legal / operations governance must eventually define:

1. recording-related purpose/scope boundaries, including capture participation, replay publication/use and separately approved promotional clips;
2. the effective boundary for withdrawal while a live recorded occurrence is still running;
3. the consequence of withdrawal after capture but before replay publication;
4. the consequence of withdrawal after replay publication;
5. the consequence where one participant withdraws from a multi-person recording and affected material is separable;
6. the consequence where affected material is materially inseparable;
7. role/context-specific withdrawal rules where a separate contractual/lawful basis may exist;
8. the fail-closed state while compliant remediation/replacement is unresolved;
9. how current replay publication/access changes without rewriting historical occurrence, attendance, prior publication or prior lawful views;
10. how dependent derivatives/uses are identified and re-evaluated;
11. that withdrawal is not Full Deletion and must not silently revoke unrelated rights;
12. that any alternative lawful basis for continued processing must come from explicit Privacy/legal authority rather than JIT invention.

This acceptance does not resolve OQ-021, retention policy or legal sufficiency.
