# 99. Pass 12 — Community participation / moderation / evidence semantics

## 99.1 Scope hard stop

This pass answers only:

1. what a Programme `community_prompt` means at the Community boundary;
2. how external Facebook participation differs from future first-party Community truth;
3. how group membership/access, viewing, posting, commenting, reacting, moderation state and Programme satisfaction differ;
4. when future first-party Community participation may be consumed as minimum Programme evidence;
5. why provider engagement metrics and external activity cannot silently become completion authority;
6. how moderation hiding/removal/editing/review affects Programme evidence boundaries;
7. how Community restrictions, suspensions, bans and appeals compose with Programme requirements;
8. how sensitive disclosures and peer-health-advice boundaries constrain programme community activities;
9. how reporter identity and restricted moderation evidence remain separated from Programmes and ordinary Analytics;
10. how entitlement end, former-member state, deletion/anonymisation and historical accepted Programme evidence compose;
11. how Community/provider outages and inaccessible participation surfaces affect participant-failure semantics;
12. how manual Community corrections and Programme exceptions remain separately authorised;
13. how challenge, Research and Voting meanings avoid being swallowed by Community or Programmes merely because they are surfaced together; and
14. whether a new Product, Architecture or Domain gap is required before later FP-008 JIT.

This pass deliberately does **not** decide:

- the exact Facebook moderation/privacy/disclosure/escalation operating procedure under `OQ-023`;
- exact moderator staffing, rota, SLA or escalation timing under `OQ-026` or operations authority;
- exact programme completion thresholds under `OQ-019` / `OQ-025`;
- exact deletion, anonymisation or restricted moderation-evidence retention durations;
- native first-party Community implementation, feed ranking, realtime mechanics or provider migration under FP-013;
- unrestricted member direct messaging, which remains excluded initially;
- exact Community Resources, actions, schemas, indexes, queues, event contracts or provider APIs;
- a Facebook sync/import/reconciliation engine;
- a generic social graph, reputation engine, engagement-score system or social-learning LMS;

# 100. Pass-12 semantic model

These are working semantic conclusions, not schema prescriptions.

## 100.1 A `community_prompt` is programme composition, not duplicated Community truth

ProgrammeVersion may define a `community_prompt`, discussion invitation or other Community-linked activity. Edition/Cohort may associate the applicable governed community context.

Keep the authorities separate:

```text
Programme activity/prompt meaning         -> Programmes & Challenges
Edition/Cohort association                -> Programmes & Challenges
current qualifying access                 -> Entitlements
native group/participation/content        -> Community
native moderation/sanction/appeal         -> Community
external Facebook/provider state          -> external operating evidence only
Programme satisfaction decision           -> Programmes & Challenges
privacy/deletion orchestration             -> Privacy & Consent
```

A shared page or prompt does not create shared-write ownership.

## 100.2 Access, presence, contribution, moderation and Programme satisfaction are not synonyms

A participant may be:

- entitled but not yet admitted to a community surface;
- admitted/member but only reading;
- able to comment but not post under group policy;
- have submitted content pending moderation;
- have a visible contribution later hidden/removed;
- temporarily restricted or suspended;
- permanently banned from Community while still enrolled in a Programme; or
- have a qualifying native participation fact that Programmes accepts under an explicit ProgrammeVersion rule.

Do not collapse these states into one `community_complete` flag.

## 100.3 FP-008 external Facebook activity is not completion authority by default

The early Facebook path validates community value and operations. Current authority does not create a canonical NewYou record for every external post/comment/reaction/view.

Therefore FP-008 must not make Facebook provider activity completion-critical merely because it can be observed manually or through provider tooling.

If Product later requires external community activity as completion evidence, that needs an explicit governed evidence route plus the applicable `OQ-019`, `OQ-023` and programme policy decisions. Pass 12 does not invent that route.

## 100.4 A community prompt may still be valuable without being completion-critical

An FP-008 day may expose a governed community prompt for discussion, encouragement, announcements or support while Programme completion relies on separately governed activities.

Do not manufacture provider-import infrastructure merely to turn every participant interaction into a Programme metric.

## 100.5 Future first-party Community may provide minimum qualifying participation evidence

When native Community exists and a Product-approved ProgrammeVersion explicitly requires a Community activity, Programmes may consume a minimum Community-owned fact sufficient to evaluate the requirement.

The safe boundary is conceptually:

```text
Community owns qualifying participation/moderation truth
        ↓
Programmes receives/reads minimum governed evidence
        ↓
Programmes records its own requirement-satisfaction decision + minimum provenance
```

Programmes does not need the post body, reporter identity, case notes or full moderation history merely to know whether an approved participation requirement was satisfied.

## 100.6 Raw content existence is not necessarily qualifying participation

`post created`, `comment created` or `reaction clicked` is not automatically programme evidence.

If a future rule genuinely requires meaningful/qualifying community participation, the governing Programme/Product contract must identify the acceptable Community-owned evidence meaning. Community moderation/safety rules remain intact; submitting prohibited content cannot be upgraded into qualifying participation merely because the provider accepted it.

No generic engagement-score or reaction-count threshold is authorised.

## 100.7 Moderation action does not directly rewrite Programme truth

Hiding, removing, editing, reviewing or sanctioning Community content remains Community truth.

If Programmes previously accepted a legitimate Community-owned qualifying fact, a later moderation correction does not silently mutate Programme history. Programmes must reconcile through an explicit rule when the correction is material, preserving before/after provenance without copying restricted moderation evidence.

Exact post-award consequences remain within the existing correction/exception framework rather than being invented here.

## 100.8 Community sanctions do not automatically equal Programme failure or exemption

A restriction, suspension or ban may make a Community surface unavailable. That state belongs to Community.

Programmes must not automatically:

- unenrol the participant;
- mark the Programme failed;
- mark the Community activity complete;
- exempt the activity;
- fabricate a substitute; or
- disclose sanction details to other participants.

If a completion-relevant Community activity ever exists, the Programme/Edition contract needs an explicit consequence or alternative path for sanctioned/restricted participants where appropriate.

## 100.9 An appeal does not require Programmes to become a moderation court

Community owns appeal eligibility, review and outcome. Programmes may need to defer/reconcile a dependent participation decision where a material source fact is unsettled, but it does not re-adjudicate the moderation case.

Correct bounded waiting or an approved non-Community path is preferable to guessing.

## 100.10 Sensitive public disclosure can never be the price of Programme completion

A Programme requirement must not force a participant to publish:

- private medical history;
- medication details;
- trauma disclosures;
- private journal/reflection content;
- plan details;
- weight/body-measurement data; or
- other highly sensitive information

merely to satisfy participation.

A safe prompt may invite optional bounded personal experience, but completion semantics must not punish a participant for declining unsafe/public disclosure.

## 100.11 Peer advice and moderation classifications are not Programme learning authority

Community moderation may remove or redirect unsafe advice. That action does not make Community the owner of clinical truth, and it does not make Programmes the owner of the moderation classification.

Safety/Professional pathways retain their existing authority where escalation is needed.

## 100.12 Reporter identity and restricted moderation evidence stay out of Programme evidence

Programme satisfaction should consume only the minimum source fact needed. Reporter identity, moderation evidence, internal case notes and appeal material are not ordinary Programme-completion payloads.

Audit/Analytics copies cannot be used as a workaround to obtain them.

## 100.13 Community entitlement end does not erase historical Programme satisfaction

When qualifying Community access ends, new entitled participation ends under Product policy. Historical Programme satisfaction already accepted under a valid rule is not silently erased merely because current Community access ended.

Conversely, Programmes cannot keep a participant active in Community by preserving an old completion record; current access still belongs to Entitlements/Community admission rules.

## 100.14 Deletion/anonymisation must not be defeated by Programme provenance

If Community content is deleted/anonymised under Privacy law, Programmes must not retain a hidden copy of the content merely to preserve completion evidence.

Where lawful and necessary, Programme provenance should be the minimum independently meaningful fact/reference allowed by the governing deletion/retention contract. If that contract requires the evidence to disappear or be de-linked, the Programme consequence needs explicit reconciliation rather than covert content retention.

Exact retention semantics remain downstream Privacy/JIT work.

## 100.15 External-provider outage or ambiguity is not participant failure

If the governed Facebook surface is unavailable, an external account/group linkage is ambiguous, provider data is lost, or moderation tooling fails, Programmes must not manufacture a failure or success.

For FP-008 this is another reason community prompts should not become completion-critical without an explicitly governed evidence and recovery route.

## 100.16 Manual corrections stay with the owning authority

A moderator may correct Community-owned participation/moderation state through Community authority where native Community exists. A Programme operator may separately apply an authorised Programme exception/substitution.

No generic `mark community complete`, `restore deleted post for completion`, or `override ban from Programme admin` power is admitted.

## 100.17 Community surfaces do not absorb Challenge, Research or Voting authority

A challenge may use Community discussion while Programmes owns challenge definition/completion. A Research instrument shown in Community remains Research & Feedback truth. A governed vote displayed in Community remains Voting & Balloting truth.

Surface location never transfers durable authority.

## 100.18 Failure and moderation evidence remain explainable without shared authority

A participant-facing dispute such as “I could not join the group”, “my post was removed”, “I was restricted” or “my participation was not recognised” may require evidence from Entitlements, Community/provider operations, Privacy and Programmes.

That coordination does not create a shared-write case owner for all underlying truths. Each owner reconciles its own state; Programmes owns only the programme consequence.
