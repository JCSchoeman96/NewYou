# 2. Routing rule: contract authority is not source-truth ownership

No single source Domain owns the cross-domain gate-metric definition merely because one of its facts appears in the numerator or denominator.

The correct separation is:

1. **Product / Roadmap authority** fixes the required outcome and any already-governed metric semantics.
2. **FP-006 JIT** must freeze the complete metric contract before first paid participation where current authority intentionally leaves details unresolved.
3. **Source Domains** retain exclusive ownership of their component business truths and lifecycle semantics.
4. **Analytics** may derive the metric only after the contract is frozen; it may not choose unresolved business semantics, manufacture lifecycle state, or select a convenient denominator.

This means “owner” in the handoff below is split deliberately into:

- **contract decision layer** — where the unresolved measurement contract must be frozen; and
- **authoritative source Domain(s)** — where the required business facts and lifecycle meanings originate.

Creating a new “metric owner” Domain or assigning the entire cross-domain contract to Analytics would create competing authority and is rejected.

# 3. Shared fail-closed rule for unresolved gate contracts

An unresolved metric definition is different from a completed metric definition whose source feed is temporarily incomplete.

Before first paid participation:

- if a required gate metric contract is still unresolved, that metric is **NOT CONTRACTED / NOT GATE-ELIGIBLE**;
- the unresolved metric must not be represented as `0%`, `100%`, pass, fail or “best available” using invented semantics;
- Analytics must not choose a default denominator, qualifying action, window, timezone, retry rule or lifecycle interpretation merely to produce a number; and
- the programme-level safe state is:

```text
PAID PARTICIPATION: BLOCKED / NO-GO
```

Once a metric contract is fully frozen, later analytical/source incompleteness follows the already accepted missing-data and freshness rules rather than reopening the definition.
