## 3. Explicit compression corrections

### CORR-01 — unsupported review claim

The old compression candidate stated that v0.32 itself received `PASS WITH NON-BLOCKING CORRECTIONS — COMPRESSION-READY`. The v0.32 source itself says it is a complete authoring candidate and not yet independently certified. No separate post-v0.32 repository review artifact was found. The compact pack therefore does **not** attribute that verdict to v0.32. It records the supplied external compression review only as external review input.

### CORR-02 — R549 authority wording

Old candidate wording implied an authoritative current allocation. Corrected wording is **accepted Pre-JIT working Product/Commercial allocation candidate, pending explicit governed promotion**. Numbers remain R249 Assessment + R300 Plan = R549; R99 discount allocated to Plan.

### CORR-03 — future General Wellness conditional clock invariant restored

Current MVP remains no timer. The compact contract restores the accepted future conditional rule: if later Product authority creates a bounded election, the clock starts only when the complete governed choice is durably available and exercisable—not from eligibility timestamp, provider state, notification acceptance, open/read telemetry, Analytics, stale UI or queue state.

### CORR-04 — foundational Operations doctrine restored

The old compressed candidate over-centred later commercial deltas. The compact contract restores owner authority, support projections, correction taxonomy, minimum-necessary access, pilot exposure vs paid-demand evidence, degradation/incidents and measurement/evidence seams.

### CORR-05 — PT provenance corrected

The deep v0.32 control source is not represented as the sole detailed source of `OPS-PT-001...470`. The Evidence Index maps `001...210` to the historical discovery files and `211...470` to exact later control-source sections.

### CORR-06 — pre-redemption authority precision

Before redemption, cancellation authority is the authorised purchaser/sponsor/commercial-order actor under the accepted offer. Refund destination remains the authoritative payer/payment relationship. Bare payer status is not automatically cancellation authority.

### CORR-07 — deletion commitment versus provider settlement

For both failed-fulfilment and still-valid ordinary pre-generation refund paths, durable Commerce refund commitment before entitlement destruction is required; completed external provider settlement is **not** required before deletion completion once obligation preservation and other deletion conditions pass.

## 4. Scope not compressed away

This compact pack remains an Operations / Support / Pilot pack. Commerce-heavy later deltas do not displace operator authority, support projections, correction/reconciliation, safe stop, release evidence, operational burden, degradation, pilot staging or cross-domain recovery.

## 5. Historical preservation

Historical discovery/control/promotion artifacts are evidence. They are relocated to `deep/archive/` without rewriting bytes. The canonical deep v0.32 source is preserved separately under `deep/`. The old compressed candidate is archived unchanged as compression provenance.
