## Method and classification rules

The evidence register below uses one row for each distinct architecture-relevant Product obligation or tightly coupled obligation cluster. A row may cite several Product sentences and several existing ARQs. Repeated cross-capability rules are retained as separate rows when they add a cross-boundary invariant rather than merely restating a local capability rule.

The primary classification is exactly one of the required values:

- `EXISTING_ARQ_SUFFICIENT` means the frozen ARQ fully expresses the obligation and no newer Product source needs to be propagated for the row.
- `EXISTING_ARQ_SOURCE_REFRESH_REQUIRED` means the frozen semantics are sufficient, but the v1.3.0 Product source must be added to the governed trace.
- `EXISTING_ARQ_AMENDMENT_REQUIRED` means the frozen ARQ is directionally relevant but does not express the complete new Product obligation.
- `NEW_ARQ_REQUIRED` means no adequate frozen ARQ represents a material Product-derived architecture obligation.
- `NO_ARQ_REQUIRED` means the rule remains Product policy, commercial policy, Roadmap, operations, UI/content detail or later implementation detail.
- `UPSTREAM_PRODUCT_CONTRADICTION` means the current Product Law cannot determine the requirement without inventing semantics. This would be a STOP condition.

The strict result below has no pure `EXISTING_ARQ_SUFFICIENT` rows. A row receives `EXISTING_ARQ_SOURCE_REFRESH_REQUIRED` only where the retained ARQs fully cover that row and the newer Product source directly supports those exact ARQs. Where a generic ARQ is valid evidence for only part of the obligation, the row uses source-refresh-plus-additive treatment or linked additive coverage instead. This is not a claim that the frozen ARQs are defective.
