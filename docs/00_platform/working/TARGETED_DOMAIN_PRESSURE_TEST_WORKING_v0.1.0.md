The 14 new-Domain rows were two lifecycles, not fourteen Domains: Research (campaign/instrument/response/annotation) and Voting (rules/submission/tally/finalisation/result/adjudication).

### 3.1 Research & Feedback

Durable Research truth is the governed study/instrument/response lifecycle, including declared participation mode, producing-version binding, correction/withdrawal/de-link, and staff annotation as a distinct evidence role.

It is not Content, Experimentation, Analytics, Health, Temperament, Community or Audit.

**Pressure-test conclusion:** existing Domain Law cannot coherently own this lifecycle.

### 3.2 Voting & Balloting

Durable Voting truth is the `ARC-332` stack except reward/entitlement consequence: rules, submission/integrity evidence, accepted tally, finalisation, official result, and adjudication.

UI location does not choose the owner. Research polls remain Research by purpose (`DEC-298`). Competitions compose multiple Domains; Voting does not own a competition as a whole.

**Pressure-test conclusion:** existing Domain Law cannot coherently own this lifecycle. Community and Programmes fail the lifecycle test.

### 3.3 Interactive Tools

Most tool use is not a durable truth. `calculation != authority`. Public/anonymous use is non-persistent by default. There is no legitimate generic Tools / Interactive Evidence owner.

Purpose-distributed:

- published editorial/presentation artifact → Content & Media (narrow);
- plan/nutrition protocol → Plans & Nutrition;
- health-fact establishment → Health Records action;
- educational/self-tracking persisted result → Habits, Journals & Progress (narrow);
- other owner actions → handoff.

Content must **not** own all tool calculations. Habits must **not** own all persisted tool results.

### 3.4 Platform Member Reference

PMR is an Account-scoped human-facing identifier assigned after successful individual Account creation. Lookup is an Identity interface. Beneficiary use is a handoff. Exact encoding is `ARQ-IAM-013` and remains unfrozen.

**Pressure-test conclusion:** Identity & Access already legitimately owns PMR as Account truth; an additive scope amendment is required. This was **not** a human Grill question.
