# 4. Accepted Pass-I semantic conclusions

1. Replay correction is already an authorised Product capability under DEC-188 and Product §21G.15.
2. The controlling Product correction vocabulary remains `minor_editorial_correction`, `material_content_correction`, `safety_correction`, `legal_or_consent_correction`, `full_withdrawal`.
3. Minor correction preserves original history; destructive historical rewrite is not acceptable.
4. Material correction creates an approved superseding version; provider/render completion is not publication authority.
5. Safety correction withdraws affected current use immediately even when no replacement is ready.
6. Legal/consent correction composes with current Privacy authority, OQ-021 and accepted `LIVE-UPD-006`; technical editability cannot substitute for authority.
7. Full withdrawal may legitimately leave no current replay.
8. Current replay publication and historical publication evidence are separate.
9. Derivative lineage must converge on the corrected/current replay.
10. Correction failure semantics depend on correction class; already-invalid safety/legal/consent V1 remains fail-closed when V2 processing fails.
11. Stale delivery capabilities, edge/cache state and provider reachability cannot become current publication authority.
12. Correction-of-correction uses successor/withdrawal semantics rather than destructive rollback.
13. Replay correction never rewrites occurrence or attendance truth; historical delivery/version evidence remains attributable to what was actually delivered.
14. No new Live Product decision package is justified by Pass I; existing Product correction classes, OQ-014, OQ-021, accepted `LIVE-UPD-006`, C&M `CM-UPD-011`, C&M JIT and executable/provider proof own the remaining work.
