# 76. Pass-9 semantic model

These are working semantic conclusions, not schema prescriptions.

## 76.1 Preserve two dimensions: requirement definition and participant-specific adjudication

The ProgrammeVersion carries the stable requirement meaning.

At evaluation time, the participant/attempt may have a distinct applicability or adjudication fact.

Conceptually:

```text
Requirement definition
  required-for-progression
  required-for-completion
  required-for-safety
  recommended
  optional
  conditional

Participant/attempt context
  applicable
  not applicable under explicit condition
  performed/satisfied by accepted evidence
  skipped/missed
  safety-blocked
  exempt under approved authority
  satisfied via approved substitute / alternate evidence
```

This is a semantic separation, **not** a mandated database enum/schema.

## 76.2 Exemption is not completion

An exemption means an approved authority has determined that the original requirement is not owed, or is non-blocking, for the specified participant/attempt/context.

It does not mean the activity was performed.

Therefore preserve:

- the original versioned requirement;
- the exemption authority/policy;
- the participant/Enrolment scope;
- why/when it applied;
- and sufficient provenance for later explanation.

Do not turn exemption into fabricated activity evidence.

## 76.3 Substitution is not source-history rewriting

A substitute means an explicitly approved different activity/evidence path may satisfy a named programme requirement.

The platform must preserve:

- the original requirement;
- the approved mapping;
- the substitute fact/evidence owner;
- the accepted satisfaction decision; and
- the fact that the original activity itself was not necessarily performed.

## 76.4 Alternate evidence is narrower than substitution

If two evidence routes prove the **same business requirement**, this is alternate evidence.

If a **different activity/requirement route** is accepted in place of the original, this is substitution/equivalence.

The distinction matters because source ownership, participant promises and completion meaning may differ.

## 76.5 `conditional` needs a governed predicate and consequence

The label `conditional` is insufficient on its own.

A publishable ProgrammeVersion must define, for each conditional activity that can affect progression/completion/safety:

- the authoritative predicate/source;
- when it is evaluated;
- whether it can be re-evaluated;
- what happens when false (for example non-applicable);
- what happens when true; and
- which progression/completion/safety consequence applies.

Do not infer this from Analytics segments, UI state or developer convention.

For Nuwe Jy, concrete rules that materially affect completion/safety remain within `OQ-019` / `OQ-025`.

## 76.6 `not applicable` preserves truth

A requirement that is not applicable under an explicit rule is not `completed`.

It can be excluded/non-blocking according to the approved completion policy while remaining historically explainable.

Its effect on denominators/thresholds must be explicit where percentages are used.

## 76.7 `skipped` and `safety-blocked` are occurrence/participant states, not satisfaction

A participant may skip an item, or Safety may block performance.

Neither state alone says the Programme requirement is satisfied.

The approved Programme/Safety policy must decide whether the requirement remains outstanding, becomes exempt, has an approved substitute, changes the programme outcome, or triggers another governed path.

## 76.8 Safety-required exception paths fail closed

No generic Programme/admin exception mechanism may override authoritative Safety restrictions or clear required safety gates.

If Product/Safety explicitly permits an alternate safe activity, exemption or other route, Programmes may apply that governed consequence while leaving Safety truth authoritative.

Absent that rule: fail closed.

## 76.9 Replay is not a universal attendance substitute

Live registration, live attendance and replay are separate Events & Live truths.

A replay can satisfy a programme requirement only if the approved ProgrammeVersion/Product policy explicitly defines:

- which live requirement may be replaced;
- what replay evidence is sufficient;
- any completion window;
- and any safety/interaction obligations that cannot be substituted.

No platform-wide “watch replay = attended live” rule is authorised.

## 76.10 Exception handling must not lie to metrics

If a completion threshold uses a denominator, approved policy must state how:

- not-applicable items;
- exempted items;
- substituted items;
- optional items; and
- conditional items

affect numerator/denominator.

Never count an exemption as a performed activity merely to reach a threshold.

Analytics consumes the Programme decision; it does not choose the treatment.

## 76.11 Exception/adjudication history is attempt-specific by default

An exemption/substitute decision normally belongs to the relevant Enrolment/requirement context.

Do not automatically copy it to:

- a repeat/restart Enrolment;
- a future ProgrammeVersion;
- a different Edition; or
- another participant.

A governing Product rule may explicitly make some decision reusable, but that must be stated rather than inferred.

## 76.12 Corrections use supersession, not silent deletion

If an exception/substitute decision is corrected:

- preserve the prior decision/history;
- record the superseding decision;
- re-evaluate current progression/completion where material;
- and keep participant-facing state truthful.

If completion/certificate has already been awarded, the remaining `PRG-GAP-002` correction policy applies.

## 76.13 Late enrolment, pause and time boundaries are not exemption engines

None of these events automatically exempts required work:

- joining late;
- pausing;
- resuming;
- cohort conclusion;
- completion deadline;
- archive date.

Catch-up/recovery policy determines the supported path; detailed mechanics remain Pass 10.

## 76.14 Generic rules/equivalence engines remain unjustified

FP-008 needs only the bounded rules actually approved for Nuwe Jy.

Do not introduce:

- arbitrary Boolean rule DSLs;
- generic waiver matrices;
- transitive equivalence graphs;
- role-authored rule builders;
- “mark complete” superpowers;
- LMS-style prerequisite/exception engines.

Add a mechanism only when a concrete approved programme rule requires it.

# 79. Pass-9 refinements to earlier working synthesis

## PRG-REF-067 — Requirement type and exception/adjudication are separate dimensions

The six DEC-153 types define intrinsic ProgrammeVersion requirement meaning. Exemption, substitution, not-applicable, skipped and safety-blocked must not be flattened into that same enum.

## PRG-REF-068 — Exemption makes a requirement non-blocking; it does not fabricate performance

Where an approved exemption applies, preserve the original requirement and record that the participant is not required to perform it for the applicable attempt/context. Do not mark the source activity completed.

## PRG-REF-069 — Substitution satisfies a requirement through an approved alternative without rewriting source truth

Preserve both original requirement and substitute evidence/provenance. Do not mark the original activity performed merely because the requirement is satisfied via substitute.

## PRG-REF-070 — Alternate evidence and substitute activity are distinct

Alternate evidence proves the same requirement through another approved evidence route. A substitute is a different activity/requirement route accepted in place of the original. Avoid collapsing them.

## PRG-REF-071 — Conditional activity needs an explicit predicate, authority and consequence

`conditional` is not self-executing. The ProgrammeVersion must define what authoritative condition activates it, when that condition is evaluated/re-evaluated, and what consequence applies when active.

## PRG-REF-072 — Not-applicable is an applicability result, not completion evidence

When an explicit conditional rule evaluates false, the item can be non-applicable. Preserve that truth distinctly rather than calling it completed or skipped.

## PRG-REF-073 — Skipped and safety-blocked preserve truthful participant/source state

Neither state by itself satisfies a requirement. Programme consequences follow the approved requirement/exception rule while source truth stays with its owner.

## PRG-REF-074 — Safety-required exceptions fail closed

No generic Programmes/admin waiver may bypass a required safety rule. Any exemption/substitute must be explicitly authorised by the relevant Product/Safety policy.

## PRG-REF-075 — Replay is not a universal attendance substitute

Events & Live owns attendance/replay truth. A replay may satisfy a programme requirement only when the approved ProgrammeVersion/OQ-025 policy explicitly says so and defines acceptable evidence.

## PRG-REF-076 — Exception policy affects completion denominators without inventing performance

Approved metrics must state how exempt/not-applicable/substituted requirements affect denominator and threshold calculations. Exemption must not be counted as performed merely to hit a percentage.

## PRG-REF-077 — Exception adjudication is Enrolment/requirement scoped unless policy says otherwise

Do not mutate ProgrammeVersion meaning or automatically carry exceptions into repeat/restart/migrated Enrolments. Applicability must remain traceable to the governing rule.

## PRG-REF-078 — Exception commands require provenance and supersession, not silent deletion

Where operator/human adjudication exists, preserve authority, reason, actor/context, effective time and supersession/reversal history as appropriate. Exact Resource/action design remains JIT.

## PRG-REF-079 — Generic waiver/equivalence/rules machinery remains YAGNI

Support only the bounded conditional predicates, approved evidence alternatives and substitution/exemption cases actually required by concrete Product-approved programmes. Do not pre-build an LMS rules DSL.
