# 92. Pass-11 semantic model

These are working semantic conclusions, not schema prescriptions.

## 92.1 A Programme live requirement references Event truth; it does not duplicate it

A ProgrammeVersion may define a live-session-related activity and its requirement type. An Edition/Cohort may link the applicable Events-owned session/occurrence.

Keep these authorities separate:

```text
ProgrammeVersion requirement meaning     -> Programmes & Challenges
Edition/Cohort session association       -> Programmes & Challenges
Event/session occurrence + schedule      -> Events & Live
registration/capacity/waitlist           -> Events & Live
attendance/check-in                      -> Events & Live
recording/replay asset publication       -> Content & Media
replay/current protected access          -> Entitlements
Programme evidence acceptance            -> Programmes & Challenges
```

The exact reference representation is JIT detail.

## 92.2 Registration, admission, attendance and satisfaction are not synonyms

A participant can be:

- entitled but not registered;
- registered but waitlisted;
- registered/confirmed but absent;
- admitted/joined but not satisfy a stronger attendance rule;
- attended under Events truth but not satisfy a different Programme requirement; or
- satisfy a Programme requirement through an explicitly approved replay/substitute path without ever being recorded as live-attended.

Do not collapse these states into one `attended/completed` flag.

## 92.3 Provider telemetry is evidence to Events/Content, not Programme authority

Provider callbacks, connection rows, stream heartbeats, player progress and dashboard status are external observations.

They may help Events reconcile occurrence/attendance or Content/operations reconcile media delivery, but Programmes must consume the owning platform truth/evidence contract rather than trusting provider telemetry directly.

## 92.4 Protected join and playback always use current access

Historic registration, a copied join URL or prior replay entitlement cannot permanently authorise protected delivery.

Before issuing protected joining/playback capability, the platform re-evaluates current applicable authority.

This preserves Pass 5: access truth may change while registration/Enrolment history remains true.

## 92.5 Capacity fairness is a configuration/operations obligation when attendance matters

If live attendance is completion/progression/safety-critical, the Edition cannot safely activate with participant capacity greater than the guaranteed/approved live path unless an explicit approved alternative exists.

A participant who was prevented from attending by platform capacity/waitlist policy must not be classified as if she voluntarily failed to participate.

Exact capacity/staffing/live ownership remains `OQ-026`.

## 92.6 Event occurrence failure does not become participant failure

Cancellation, postponement, provider outage, force majeure or material format change remain Events/operational truth.

Programmes must not:

- manufacture attendance;
- mark the participant failed by default;
- silently exempt the requirement;
- silently substitute replay; or
- silently rewrite the ProgrammeVersion requirement.

The approved Programme/Edition policy determines the consequence. For Nuwe Jy, that decision belongs the existing `OQ-025` / `OQ-026` gate family and Pass-9 exception framework.

## 92.7 Replay is a governed alternative surface, not live attendance

A replay is not an attendance record.

Where Product/Programme policy permits replay to satisfy a requirement, preserve the distinction:

```text
original live requirement
+ source occurrence truth
+ approved substitution/evidence rule
+ governed replay identity/version
+ replay/current access
+ accepted evidence
-> Programme satisfaction decision
```

Do not rewrite the participant as live-attended.

## 92.8 Replay substitution must define evidence and any non-substitutable obligations

An approved replay path must state enough to avoid implementation invention:

- which live requirement is being satisfied/replaced;
- whether this is alternate evidence or a substitute activity;
- the acceptable evidence source/contract;
- the allowed window/scope;
- whether an acknowledgement/reflection/check-in is required; and
- which live-only interaction/safety obligations cannot be replaced.

No universal player percentage, watch-time or start event is authorised.

## 92.9 Recording existence, approved replay and replay access are three different things

Provider capture may exist while replay publication is prohibited, delayed or awaiting editing/consent review.

An approved replay may exist while a specific participant lacks current replay access.

An Entitlement may exist while the replay itself is withdrawn/unpublished.

All three conditions must be respected before protected replay delivery.

## 92.10 Replay availability must compose with the promised programme window

If replay is the approved recovery/substitution path for a completion-relevant live session, its approved publication/access/expiry needs to cover the participant window promised by the Programme/Edition, or the contract must define another approved route.

A mismatch is a configuration/activation or incident-policy problem, not participant non-compliance.

## 92.11 Replay correction/withdrawal preserves historical evidence provenance

Content correction/withdrawal governs future replay delivery.

Where an earlier approved replay/version legitimately satisfied a programme requirement, later withdrawal/correction does not silently erase the historical Programme decision. Preserve enough provenance for reconciliation and route material post-award consequence through `PRG-GAP-002`.

## 92.12 Late enrolment and pause do not create automatic live exceptions

A late enrollee may join after a prior live occurrence; a paused participant may miss or even validly attend an occurrence.

Neither state automatically means:

- exempt;
- replay-substituted;
- failed;
- restarted; or
- shifted to a private event schedule.

The approved Programme/Edition exception/recovery policy decides the path while Events truth remains intact.

## 92.13 Group Q&A remains educational and bounded

Programme composition may link group Q&A/live discussion, but the Programme may not require private diagnosis, prescription or treatment in the group setting.

Questions needing private clinical/professional handling route to the appropriate professional pathway. Chat/comments do not become Community or Programme evidence merely because they occur during the session.

## 92.14 Included Nuwe Jy live sessions do not imply event-ticket commerce

FP-007 and `DEC-279` deliberately prove governed live/replay before full event commerce.

For an included cohort live session, use only the registration/capacity/access semantics actually required. Do not manufacture TicketType/payment-hold/refund machinery unless the concrete session is separately scarce/ticketed and the relevant FP-015/event-commerce authority is activated.

## 92.15 Live-session incidents must remain explainable across owners

For participant-facing disputes such as "I could not join", "I was waitlisted", "the stream failed" or "my attendance is missing", explanation may require coordinated evidence from Events, Entitlements, Communications, provider evidence and Programmes.

That does not create a shared-write incident authority. Each Domain retains its truth; Programmes owns only the programme consequence after the source facts are reconciled.

# 95. Pass-11 refinements to earlier working synthesis

## PRG-REF-095 - Programme live-session activity is a reference/requirement, not an Event duplicate

ProgrammeVersion may define a live-session-related activity/requirement and Edition/Cohort may link the relevant Event occurrence. Events & Live retains schedule/registration/capacity/attendance mutation authority.

## PRG-REF-096 - Registration, admission, attendance and Programme satisfaction are separate facts

A registered or seat-holding participant is not automatically attended; an attended participant is not automatically Programme-satisfied unless the ProgrammeVersion accepts that evidence.

## PRG-REF-097 - Provider/player telemetry remains evidence, not live/replay business authority

Provider join/leave/watch callbacks are reconciled by the owning platform Domain. They do not directly mark Programme attendance/completion.

## PRG-REF-098 - Capacity denial cannot be participant non-compliance

If completion-critical live attendance is required, Edition activation must provide adequate capacity or an explicit governed alternative. Waitlist/capacity exclusion is platform configuration/operations truth, not a participant failure.

## PRG-REF-099 - Occurrence cancellation/postponement/format change needs an explicit Programme consequence

Events owns the occurrence transition. Programmes must apply an approved requirement/recovery/exception consequence and may not fabricate attendance or silently rewrite the activity definition.

## PRG-REF-100 - Replay substitution is explicit, scoped and evidence-defined

A replay can satisfy a live requirement only under explicit ProgrammeVersion/Product policy covering the replaced requirement, acceptable evidence, window/scope and non-substitutable interaction/safety obligations.

## PRG-REF-101 - Replay occurrence association, publication and access remain distinct authorities

Events owns session/occurrence association; Content & Media owns replay asset/publication/version; Entitlements owns protected replay access; Programmes owns programme satisfaction.

## PRG-REF-102 - Protected joining/playback rechecks current access

Historic registration does not permanently authorise a join link or replay. Joining instructions and playback remain protected by current platform authority.

## PRG-REF-103 - Provider recording existence is not governed replay availability

A captured file is not automatically a published replay. Recording notice/consent, editing, rights, publication, correction/withdrawal and replay entitlement remain governed.

## PRG-REF-104 - Historical replay satisfaction preserves exact provenance

Where replay evidence is accepted, preserve enough provenance to explain which approved replay/version/evidence route satisfied which ProgrammeVersion requirement without copying provider/media authority into Programmes.

## PRG-REF-105 - Recovery does not auto-exempt or auto-substitute missed live sessions

Late enrolment, pause, provider outage or ordinary absence may create a recovery need, but replay/substitute/exemption treatment must come from explicit policy.

## PRG-REF-106 - Late-enrolment live obligations require Edition-specific approved treatment

A participant joining after a past live occurrence is not automatically failed or exempt. The Edition/Programme contract must define the supported path if that occurrence matters.

## PRG-REF-107 - Programme pause never rewrites Event occurrence time

A participant-specific programme pause does not shift/cancel the linked Event. Attendance occurring during pause remains Events truth and may be reconciled.

## PRG-REF-108 - Included cohort live does not imply full event commerce

FP-007/DEC-279 deliberately establish live/replay before FP-015 ticket commerce. Add holds/tickets/payment-specific event machinery only for a concrete scarce/ticketed requirement.

## PRG-REF-109 - Live/Q&A remains bounded by educational, privacy and professional-care law

Programme requirements cannot turn group Q&A into private diagnosis/treatment or provider chat into completion evidence. The live surface does not create a new shared Programme/Community/Professional-Care authority.
