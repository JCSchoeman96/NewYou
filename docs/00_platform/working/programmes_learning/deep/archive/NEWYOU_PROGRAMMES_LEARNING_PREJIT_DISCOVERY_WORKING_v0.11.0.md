# 84. Pass-10 semantic model

These are working semantic conclusions, not schema prescriptions.

## 84.1 Recovery preserves one attempt; it does not manufacture a restart

Recovery operates inside the current Enrolment attempt. It preserves accepted progress/history and helps the participant continue from current authoritative state.

A restart is separate Product semantics: it creates a new linked Enrolment. Never reset or overwrite an attempt to simulate recovery or restart.

## 84.2 “Missed work” is a recovery descriptor, not a new requirement type or terminal outcome

A useful recovery projection may identify released/applicable unfinished work, but `missed` must not become a peer requirement classification or failure state.

The projection must distinguish at least conceptually between:

- work released before an approved late enrollee joined;
- work the participant had an opportunity to do but did not complete;
- work blocked by Safety/access/content authority;
- non-applicable/exempt work; and
- optional/recommended work.

This avoids blame and prevents the recovery list from becoming false business truth.

## 84.3 The shared Edition calendar remains authoritative during recovery

Recovery never changes what cohort day it is.

For Nuwe Jy:

- released dates remain Edition truth;
- a late participant joins the current day;
- a delayed/paused participant resumes into the current day;
- catch-up is layered around that shared rhythm;
- no private participant-relative 60-day clock is silently created.

## 84.4 The next-smallest-useful-action is derived guidance

The recommendation is a participant-facing projection from current authority, not a durable source of Programme truth.

It must respect:

- current Edition day/release state;
- explicit progression/safety gates;
- requirement applicability/adjudication;
- current accepted source evidence;
- current Entitlement/access;
- current Content availability/withdrawal; and
- approved substitute/exception policy.

It may change when any of those authorities change.

## 84.5 Recovery prioritisation is bounded, not a generic adaptive curriculum

Product requires the experience to avoid dumping all missed work onto the participant immediately and to recommend the next smallest useful action.

A safe bounded policy may prioritise:

1. unresolved safety/foundation/progression gates needed for current participation;
2. the smallest currently useful required action;
3. current-day context;
4. completion-required backlog within applicable windows; and
5. optional/recommended work later or as capacity permits.

This ordering is a working constraint, not a universal LMS scheduling algorithm. Concrete FP-008 JIT may refine ordering so long as Product invariants remain true.

## 84.6 Rescheduling changes recovery targets, not historical release authority

“Reschedule unfinished activities” may mean changing a participant-facing target/recommended date/order for unfinished work inside approved recovery policy.

It must not silently change:

- Edition release dates/timezone;
- source occurrence timestamps;
- live Event schedule owned by Events & Live;
- ProgrammeVersion requirement meaning; or
- completion/catch-up boundaries without explicit authority.

## 84.7 Simplification reduces burden without fabricating satisfaction

“Simplify upcoming workload” may safely include:

- smaller presentation chunks;
- prioritising fewer actions at a time;
- deprioritising optional/recommended work;
- suppressing non-essential nudges; and
- choosing the smallest useful next action.

It must not silently:

- remove required work;
- lower completion thresholds;
- mark skipped work completed;
- create an exemption/substitute; or
- bypass Safety.

Those are separate governed decisions.

## 84.8 Enrolment pause preserves the attempt and does not pause the Edition

Minimum working meaning:

- same Enrolment remains authoritative;
- prior evidence/progress remains intact;
- shared Edition release calendar continues;
- current Entitlement is unchanged merely because the Enrolment is paused;
- no automatic completion-deadline extension occurs;
- guided nudges/recovery expectations may be suppressed according to policy;
- source evidence that validly occurs while paused remains source truth and may be reconciled.

Exact UI actions permitted while paused are JIT interaction policy, but JIT may not contradict these invariants.

## 84.9 Resume re-evaluates current authority rather than restoring stale state

On resume:

1. preserve prior progress;
2. read current Edition day;
3. read current Entitlement/access;
4. read current Safety/content status;
5. reconcile accepted source evidence;
6. identify current actionable backlog; and
7. recommend the next useful recovery action.

Resume does not shift the Edition clock backwards or recreate the old Today view as current truth.

## 84.10 Reminder pause is not Enrolment pause

Product allows recovery to pause reminders.

That is a Communications consequence/preference, not proof that the Enrolment is paused and not authority to change release/access/progress.

Likewise, resuming reminders does not itself resume a paused Enrolment.

## 84.11 Catch-up may outlive cohort conclusion but is not indefinite

Product explicitly allows formal cohort conclusion while approved individual catch-up remains open.

The Edition carries a catch-up window/completion deadline. Ordinary recovery operates within the approved boundaries.

No generic rule authorises unlimited extension beyond the catch-up window. If a future Product policy wants participant-specific extension, that must be an explicit authorised exception rather than a developer convention.

## 84.12 Deadline/window end triggers governed evaluation, not automatic punishment

At a completion deadline or catch-up-window boundary:

- do not reset progress;
- do not automatically mark `abandoned` merely because time elapsed;
- do not silently exempt outstanding work;
- apply the approved completion/outcome policy;
- preserve history and participant-facing explanation.

Any extension or exceptional continuation must be explicitly authorised.

## 84.13 Recovery cannot extend Entitlements

If current access is absent, protected recovery actions fail closed while progress/history remain.

Restoring access re-evaluates the same Enrolment/recovery context. Recovery itself never grants/extends/consumes commercial rights.

## 84.14 Recovery cannot bypass Safety

Safety-blocked work is not ordinary overdue work.

Recovery must not recommend a blocked action merely to clear backlog. If Safety later permits the action or Product/Safety approves an alternative, recompute recovery from the new authoritative state.

## 84.15 Recovery cannot repair missing/withdrawn required content by itself

If required content is withdrawn with no approved replacement, recovery cannot auto-waive, auto-substitute or mark it complete.

`PRG-GAP-011` remains the governing unresolved consequence.

## 84.16 Recovery consumes Pass-9 exception decisions; it does not create them

If a requirement is explicitly:

- not applicable;
- exempt; or
- satisfied by an approved substitute,

the recovery projection should stop nagging for the original action as still owed, while preserving truthful explanation/provenance.

Recovery must not invent those adjudications simply because the backlog is large.

## 84.17 Recovery projections should be recomputable; durable consequences remain bounded

The platform may need durable facts such as:

- Enrolment pause/resume;
- an approved participant-specific target date where policy requires it;
- reminder suppression intent; or
- an authorised exception/supersession.

But a full durable “recovery plan state machine” is not yet justified. Backlog ordering/recommendation can remain derived where correctness permits.

## 84.18 Recovery must be repeat-safe and explainable

Duplicate/retried processing must not:

- create duplicate target dates or reminder intents;
- duplicate completion evidence;
- re-open already resolved work incorrectly; or
- erase the reason a participant is seeing a particular recovery path.

Exact transactional/async design remains JIT and later concurrency pressure testing.

# 87. Pass-10 refinements to earlier working synthesis

## PRG-REF-080 — Recovery continues the same Enrolment; restart is a different operation

Ordinary delay/catch-up/resume preserve the current Enrolment attempt. A true restart creates a new linked attempt and never deletes prior history.

## PRG-REF-081 — Recovery backlog is derived, not a new source of business truth

Missed/unfinished/catch-up lists are projections over Programme requirements, Edition timing and source evidence. They must be recomputable and must not outlive corrections as competing authority.

## PRG-REF-082 — Pre-enrolment catch-up backlog must not be framed as participant failure

For approved late enrolment, previously released content may require catch-up, but the participant did not “fail” those earlier cohort days.

## PRG-REF-083 — Next-smallest-useful-action is guidance constrained by authority

The recommendation may change as evidence/access/safety changes. It must respect explicit prerequisites, current Safety, current Content availability and current Entitlement access.

## PRG-REF-084 — Rescheduling changes participant recovery targets, not Edition release history

Recovery may set/revise target dates or ordering for unfinished work inside approved policy. It cannot silently rewrite the shared Edition calendar or source occurrence timestamps.

## PRG-REF-085 — Simplification reduces cognitive/workload pressure without weakening obligations

Simplification may chunk presentation, prioritise essential work and deprioritise optional/recommended items. Changing requiredness, evidence or thresholds is an exception/versioning decision, not simplification.

## PRG-REF-086 — Pause preserves progress and does not freeze the Edition clock

A paused Enrolment remains the same durable attempt. Pause does not delete evidence, shift shared release dates, extend deadlines automatically or change Entitlement by itself.

## PRG-REF-087 — Reminder pause is separate from Enrolment pause

Programmes may request/signal reminder suppression as recovery policy, while Communications owns delivery. Muting reminders does not change release, access, progress or Enrolment lifecycle unless a separate pause action occurs.

## PRG-REF-088 — Resume re-enters current authority, not stale pre-pause context

On resume, re-evaluate current Edition day, current access, current Safety/content state and accepted evidence, then derive a recovery path from what remains.

## PRG-REF-089 — Catch-up may continue after cohort conclusion only within approved policy

The cohort may conclude while individual recovery remains open. That does not authorise indefinite catch-up or automatic extension beyond the configured/approved boundary.

## PRG-REF-090 — Time boundaries trigger evaluation, not automatic failure

Completion deadline/catch-up-window end/archive are policy boundaries. They do not by themselves fabricate `abandoned`, failure, exemption or completion outcomes.

## PRG-REF-091 — Recovery cannot become entitlement or safety override

Recovery preserves/supports participation; it cannot extend access rights, bypass Safety, or substitute withdrawn/blocked work without explicit owning-domain/Product authority.

## PRG-REF-092 — Recovery respects Pass-9 exception truth

Exempt/not-applicable requirements should not be repeatedly nagged as owed work; substitutes may be recommended only when explicitly approved; skipped/safety-blocked are not silently completed.

## PRG-REF-093 — Exact recovery ranking/UX does not require a generic adaptive-learning engine

FP-008 needs a bounded, testable recovery policy consistent with Product invariants. A generic AI/adaptive curriculum, prerequisite solver or task-planning engine remains unjustified.

## PRG-REF-094 — Durable recovery consequences are repeat-safe and explainable

If recovery creates durable target dates, reminder intents or adjudications, repeated execution must not duplicate effects, and the business reason/provenance must remain explainable. Exact Resource/action design remains JIT.
