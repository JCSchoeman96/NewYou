### 1.1 Existing frozen ownership boundary

Working discovery preserves the current Domain Law split:

- **Temperament** owns assessment methodology/version under governing methodology authority; assessment attempt; saved/submitted answers; raw scores/result; provenance; reviewed interpretation history; selected current temperament profile; and assessment-report provenance/version relationship.
- **Entitlements** owns entitlement identity, scope, source/provenance, validity/expiry/revocation and credit truth.
- A cross-domain assessment start may create coordinated consequences in both Domains, but neither Domain may silently become owner of the other's durable truth.

### 1.2 Existing assessment invariants retained

This discovery does not reopen the following unless later pressure tests expose an upstream contradiction:

- one active attempt at a time;
- all required scored questions are required for successful final submission;
- submitted answers, calculated scores and producing methodology/version are immutable while retained;
- duplicate/retried submission may not create multiple authoritative completed results;
- later digital results append provenance and do not erase prior declared or digital history;
- a newly completed result does not automatically become the selected current temperament profile;
- report/render/delivery failure does not authorise rescoring;
- exact digital scores and paid digital report are reserved for digital-assessment provenance;
- self-reported/book-derived declaration does not itself consume the included digital-assessment right;
- proprietary methodology details remain methodology-owned and may not be invented by platform engineering.

# 2. Upstream contradictions / amendment requirements discovered so far

## AT-UPD-001 — Entitlement-consumption Product Law contradiction

**Status:** `UPSTREAM PRODUCT LAW AMENDMENT REQUIRED`

Current locked DEC-055 says the assessment entitlement is not consumed until the first answer is saved and is then marked used once the attempt begins.

However current `00_PLATFORM_v1.6.0` temperament-provenance matrix states that a digitally assessed included credit is consumed only after successful assessment delivery, and later digital completion is consumed when successfully delivered.

Current Roadmap FP-003 likewise states in its exit condition that the included credit remains unused until successful digital assessment delivery.

These meanings cannot all govern the same entitlement lifecycle.

**Accepted working direction:** preserve the DEC-055 semantic: consumption occurs at first durably accepted scored answer / authoritative attempt start. Align Platform Product Law and Roadmap explicitly. Do not silently treat the working choice as already-governed law.

## AT-UPD-002 — Temperament methodology-authority wording alignment

**Status:** `UPSTREAM DOMAIN-LAW ALIGNMENT REQUIRED`

Current Product Law DEC-309 establishes that methodology authority comes from the governing IP agreement and that technical administration, including Super Admin status alone, does not create methodology authority.

The current Temperament Domain profile contains older methodology-authority wording that must not override DEC-309. Any future Domain Map successor should align the wording while preserving the existing Temperament ownership boundary.

## AT-UPD-003 — Subscription initial-assessment fulfilment semantics absent from current Product Law

**Status:** `UPSTREAM PRODUCT LAW AMENDMENT REQUIRED`

Current Product Law distinguishes purchase eligibility, unused ordinary paid assessment credit, completed attempts, annual retake interval and the separate Premium annual reassessment credit. A qualifying plan may include an assessment credit.

It does not yet define the accepted working model that a qualifying active subscription promises one included qualifying **completed** initial digital assessment and may therefore permit a replacement attempt after an incomplete attempt expires while that inclusion remains unfulfilled.

This model must be explicitly governed upstream before FP-003 can rely on it.

# 3. Accepted working-design register

## AT-WD-001 — First-answer consumption boundary

**Accepted:** `2026-10-08`

The commercial assessment right is consumed when the **first scored answer is durably accepted** into the participant's authoritative assessment attempt.

Consequences:

- opening or viewing the assessment does not consume credit;
- clicking a UI `Start` control alone does not consume credit;
- ordinary abandonment after the first accepted answer does not restore credit;
- ordinary 30-day attempt expiry does not restore credit;
- result/report delivery is a separate lifecycle and does not determine whether the attempt right was used;
- genuine technical failure may trigger a governed recovery/replacement path, but recovery must not falsify the original historical consumption event.

This is a working resolution of AT-UPD-001 and is not yet an amendment to Product Law.

## AT-WD-002 — Authoritative Assessment Attempt starts at first accepted scored answer

**Accepted:** `2026-10-08`

No authoritative Assessment Attempt exists merely because the participant opened the assessment or clicked Start.

The first durably accepted scored answer is the authoritative attempt-start boundary. At that boundary:

- exactly one Assessment Attempt begins;
- the governed assessment/methodology context required for reproducibility is pinned;
- the first answer becomes durable;
- the associated assessment credit is consumed under AT-WD-001;
- the fixed attempt lifetime begins.

Before the first accepted scored answer there is eligibility to start, but no authoritative started attempt and no right to keep a subsequently retired assessment version alive merely because a browser page was opened.

If a version retires before the first answer is authoritatively accepted, a stale first-answer request against that no-longer-startable version must fail closed rather than create an old-version attempt.

If the first answer was accepted before retirement, the already-active short-lived attempt may finish its pinned version under existing version-immutability law.

## AT-WD-003 — First-answer cross-domain commitment invariant

**Accepted:** `2026-10-08`

The first-answer business commitment has two separately owned authoritative consequences:

- Temperament: exactly one canonical Assessment Attempt starts and the first scored answer is accepted;
- Entitlements: exactly one eligible assessment credit/right is consumed.

Ownership remains separate. Temperament does not own commercial credit truth, and Entitlements does not own the assessment attempt.

The platform must nevertheless converge after crash, retry or concurrency so the settled authoritative state corresponds to the same first-answer commitment.

**Prohibited settled states:**

1. authoritative started attempt / accepted first answer while the required credit remains reusable;
2. consumed credit with no recoverable authoritative attempt/first-answer commitment corresponding to that consumption;
3. two attempts from one first-answer race;
4. two consumptions for one canonical attempt.

The semantic requirement is fail-closed and implementation-neutral. This Pre-JIT discovery does not yet select a transaction, outbox, event, queue, Ash action structure or other coordination mechanism.

## AT-WD-004 — Fixed 30-day attempt lifetime

**Accepted:** `2026-10-08`

An unfinished Assessment Attempt has a fixed 30-day completion window beginning at first-answer commitment.

- later answer saves do not reset or slide the deadline;
- the deadline remains fixed even if the producing methodology/version later retires;
- if successful submission has not occurred before the deadline, the attempt becomes terminally expired for ordinary participant use;
- stale/offline writes after expiry must not resurrect the attempt;
- expiry preserves historical attempt/answer/version/consumption evidence subject to future retention/deletion law;
- ordinary expiry does not restore consumed credit;
- genuine technical-failure recovery must be explicit and governed rather than silently extending or resurrecting the original attempt.

### AT-WD-004A — Unfinished-assessment notification obligation

The participant must be clearly informed that an unfinished assessment exists and that it will expire.

Required working semantics:

- the exact authoritative expiry deadline must be visible after attempt start;
- the product must provide a persistent in-product route to continue the active assessment;
- proactive advance-expiry notification is mandatory;
- saved/progress state must be clear enough that a participant can understand that progress is durable and resumable;
- failed notification delivery must not silently move the Temperament expiry deadline or make Communications authority over attempt validity.

**Exact reminder cadence remains open.** A proposed initial cadence of 7 days, 3 days and 24 hours before expiry has been discussed but has not yet been accepted as frozen working semantics.

## AT-WD-005 — Standalone versus subscription initial-assessment semantics

**Accepted:** `2026-10-08`

### AT-WD-005A — Standalone / once-off paid assessment

A standalone purchase provides one ordinary paid assessment right.

Before first-answer commitment:

- the unused paid right remains available unless separately refunded, revoked or otherwise governed;
- this discovery does **not** impose an arbitrary 30-day expiry on a never-started standalone paid right.

After first-answer commitment:

- the right is consumed;
- the attempt receives its own fixed 30-day lifetime;
- successful completion yields the qualifying immutable digital result/report path;
- ordinary abandonment/expiry produces no automatic free replacement;
- genuine technical-failure recovery remains separately governed.

### AT-WD-005B — Qualifying subscription initial included assessment

The accepted working commercial meaning is **one included qualifying completed initial digital assessment**, not merely one chance to start.

While all of the following remain true:

- the qualifying subscription is active and in good standing;
- the initial included digital assessment remains unfulfilled;
- no other Assessment Attempt is active;

an earlier incomplete attempt that reached terminal expiry may permit a new attempt opportunity.

Each replacement attempt:

- is a distinct historical attempt;
- starts only at its own first durably accepted scored answer;
- has its own pinned governed assessment context;
- has its own fixed 30-day lifetime;
- does not erase or reverse prior expired-attempt history;
- does not create another completed assessment entitlement if the initial inclusion has already been fulfilled.

Once one qualifying digital assessment successfully completes, the subscription's initial included-assessment obligation is fulfilled and no further replacement attempts arise from that initial inclusion.

This is **not** an entitlement to unlimited completed assessments.

### AT-WD-005C — Subscription ends during an already-started attempt

If the subscription was eligible when the attempt authoritatively began, later subscription termination does not retroactively invalidate that already-started attempt.

The attempt may continue only until its existing fixed 30-day deadline.

After subscription eligibility ends:

- no new replacement attempt arises from the ended subscription;
- if the already-started attempt expires incomplete, the expired attempt remains history and there is no subscription-funded replacement unless a later qualifying commercial offer independently establishes one.

### AT-WD-005D — Premium annual reassessment remains separate

The initial subscription assessment inclusion is distinct from the existing Premium annual reassessment entitlement.

The working model must not:

- consume the future Premium annual reassessment merely by fulfilling the initial included assessment;
- turn failed initial attempts into completed reassessments;
- allow replacement attempts for the initial inclusion to accumulate as reassessment credits;
- override the existing annual eligibility/nonaccumulation/membership-end rules for Premium reassessment.

# 4. Pressure-test ledger — v0.1.0

## AT-PT-001 — Participant answers, abandons and expires

**Scenario:** standalone participant saves 12 scored answers, abandons the assessment and reaches Day 30 without successful submission.

**Expected:** credit remains consumed; attempt becomes terminally expired; no result/report exists; no automatic replacement; historical progress remains subject to retention/deletion law.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-001 + AT-WD-004 + AT-WD-005A.

## AT-PT-002 — Opened page but never answered; version retires

**Scenario:** participant opens V1, submits no answer, V1 retires, then stale browser tries to save first V1 answer.

**Expected:** no V1 attempt existed; stale first-answer start fails closed; participant must start against a currently startable governed version.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-002.

## AT-PT-003 — Started V1 immediately before retirement

**Scenario:** first V1 answer is durably accepted before V1 retirement; V1 retires shortly afterwards.

**Expected:** the active short-lived attempt retains its pinned V1 context and may finish within its fixed lifetime.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-002 + existing DEC-067 semantics.

## AT-PT-004 — Entitlement consumed but first answer/attempt appears lost after crash

**Scenario:** cross-domain first-answer operation crashes after one owner has durably acted.

**Expected:** the platform may experience transient uncertainty internally but may not settle with consumed credit and no corresponding recoverable canonical first-answer commitment. Retry/reconciliation converges on the same business commitment.

**Result:** `PASS AS REQUIRED INVARIANT`; technical mechanism deferred.

## AT-PT-005 — First answer accepted but credit appears reusable

**Scenario:** Temperament has accepted the first answer but Entitlements reports the credit as reusable.

**Expected:** the first-answer operation cannot be treated as successfully settled. Platform fails closed and reconciles; it may not expose a legitimate active assessment plus reusable consumed-required credit.

**Result:** `PASS AS REQUIRED INVARIANT`; technical mechanism deferred.

## AT-PT-006 — Browser timeout then retry

**Scenario:** first-answer request actually succeeded but browser timed out, then participant retries.

**Expected:** retry resolves to the already-canonical attempt and already-consumed right; no second attempt and no second consumption.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-003.

## AT-PT-007 — Simultaneous first answer from two devices

**Scenario:** phone and laptop race while the participant has no active attempt and one eligible right.

**Expected:** exactly one authoritative attempt wins and exactly one right is consumed. Competing request must converge to or be rejected against the canonical attempt according to later answer-conflict semantics. It may not create Attempt A + Attempt B.

**Result:** `PASS AS REQUIRED INVARIANT`; detailed answer-conflict policy remains open.

## AT-PT-008 — Simultaneous first-answer requests against different versions

**Scenario:** stale device submits V1 while another device submits current V2.

**Expected:** only one attempt-start operation can become authoritative. The winning attempt pins one governed context. The losing request cannot change the pinned version or create a second attempt.

**Result:** `PASS AS REQUIRED INVARIANT`.

## AT-PT-009 — Day-29 activity attempts to extend expiry

**Scenario:** participant saves another answer on Day 29.

**Expected:** original deadline remains fixed. No rolling 30-day extension.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-004.

## AT-PT-010 — Expiry warning delivery fails

**Scenario:** in-product/email/push reminder fails to deliver, but authoritative deadline is reached.

**Expected:** attempt still expires at its fixed Temperament deadline. Notification failure is evidence/operational failure, not authority to extend the attempt. Product must still provide the required notification strategy and evidence appropriate to its channel contract.

**Result:** `PASS`; exact notification cadence/evidence still open.

## AT-PT-011 — Standalone buyer never starts

**Scenario:** participant buys a standalone assessment but never saves a first answer.

**Expected:** no attempt exists and the paid right remains unused/available unless separately refunded, revoked or governed. No 30-day attempt clock exists.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-005A; requires confirmation against future commercial/refund/retention policy.

## AT-PT-012 — Standalone attempt expires

**Scenario:** standalone participant starts, then expires incomplete.

**Expected:** no automatic free replacement. A genuinely technical failure may enter explicit recovery; ordinary abandonment does not.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-005A.

## AT-PT-013 — Active subscriber's first attempt expires incomplete

**Scenario:** qualifying subscriber remains active/good-standing; initial included assessment is still unfulfilled; first attempt expires incomplete.

**Expected:** expired attempt remains immutable history; subscriber may become eligible to start another attempt; prior consumption/history is not reversed.

**Result:** `PASS` under accepted working model; **upstream Product Law amendment required** via AT-UPD-003.

## AT-PT-014 — Subscriber repeatedly lets attempts expire

**Scenario:** active subscriber repeatedly starts and abandons attempts without ever completing one.

**Expected:** only one active attempt at a time; every attempt remains separately historical; no additional completed result/report is created; replacement eligibility persists only while subscription remains eligible and the one initial included completed assessment remains unfulfilled.

**Result:** `PASS WITH COMMERCIAL-ABUSE FOLLOW-UP`; rate/abuse controls must not silently redefine entitlement truth.

## AT-PT-015 — Subscriber completes the included initial assessment

**Scenario:** after one or more expired attempts, a later attempt successfully completes.

**Expected:** exactly one new authoritative digital result for that completed attempt; initial subscription inclusion becomes fulfilled; no more replacement attempts arise from that inclusion.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-005B.

## AT-PT-016 — Subscription ends while attempt is active

**Scenario:** participant validly starts while eligible, then subscription ends before the fixed attempt deadline.

**Expected:** the already-started attempt may continue to its pre-existing deadline; no replacement attempt arises after subscription eligibility ends.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-005C; requires upstream Product Law codification.

## AT-PT-017 — Premium annual reassessment collision

**Scenario:** participant has fulfilled initial subscription assessment and later qualifies for Premium annual reassessment.

**Expected:** Premium reassessment is a separate entitlement/lifecycle; initial inclusion does not consume it and expired initial attempts do not accumulate into reassessment credits.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-005D + existing DEC-035/DEC-303 separation.

# 5. Current lifecycle sketch — working only

This is semantic navigation, not an implementation state-machine specification.

## 5.1 Standalone

`eligible unused paid right`
→ first scored answer durably accepted
→ `active attempt + consumed right + pinned governed context + fixed deadline`
→ either:

- `successfully submitted/completed` → immutable result path; or
- `expired incomplete` → terminal attempt history, no ordinary replacement.

## 5.2 Qualifying subscription initial assessment

`active qualifying subscription + initial assessment inclusion unfulfilled`
→ first scored answer durably accepted
→ `active attempt + attempt consumption history + fixed deadline`
→ either:

- `successfully submitted/completed` → inclusion fulfilled; or
- `expired incomplete` → if subscription still eligible and inclusion unfulfilled, a later new attempt may be started.

Subscription termination removes future replacement eligibility but does not retroactively cancel an already-validly-started attempt before its fixed deadline.

# 7. Open-question queue for next passes

## AT-OI-001 — Technical-failure recovery semantics

What exactly constitutes a genuine technical failure, who may determine it, and does recovery:

- extend the same active attempt;
- issue a successor attempt;
- restore/regrant an entitlement consequence;
- or choose among these according to failure timing?

The answer must preserve history and never silently mutate an expired/submitted attempt back to editable.

## AT-OI-002 — Attempt submission commitment boundary

Define the exact semantic cutover between:

- editable saved progress;
- participant final-submit intent;
- accepted complete submission;
- deterministic scoring;
- canonical result issuance.

Pressure-test crashes between each boundary and duplicate/concurrent final-submit requests.

## AT-OI-003 — Active-attempt answer concurrency

Define same-question and different-question writes from multiple tabs/devices, stale revisions and last-write/conflict semantics without losing participant intent or making autosave dangerous.

## AT-OI-004 — Notification policy detail

Freeze exact required warning cadence/channel hierarchy and evidence expectations while keeping notification delivery non-authoritative over Temperament expiry.

## AT-OI-005 — Subscription replacement abuse/fair-use boundary

Determine whether the accepted replacement-until-first-completion model needs bounded abuse controls, and ensure any such controls do not silently redefine the promised initial completed-assessment inclusion.

## AT-OI-006 — Resubscription after unfulfilled initial assessment

If a subscription ends before first successful completion and the participant later buys a new qualifying subscription, determine whether the new commercial offer establishes a fresh initial-assessment inclusion, and under what product/provenance rules.

## AT-OI-007 — Version and language provenance detail

Separate methodology version, question/config version, governed content/translation version and participant-facing locale binding sufficiently to reproduce historical assessment/result/report semantics without conflating Content & Media authority with Temperament methodology authority.

## AT-OI-008 — Correction / invalidation / supersession model

Define how genuine scoring/config/content defects, exceptional reviewed interpretation and later corrected outputs append evidence without overwriting submitted answers/raw result history.

## AT-OI-009 — Result/current-profile/reassessment succession

Pressure-test multiple declared and digital results, participant selection of current profile, materially different later digital result, annual retake rules and plan-regeneration consequences.

## AT-OI-010 — Report lifecycle

Distinguish result existence, report snapshot/version, report render, participant availability/access and external delivery. Freeze retry and correction semantics without making delivery determine scoring truth.

## AT-OI-011 — Identity merge / deletion / retention

Pressure-test account reconciliation, full deletion, legal retention, participant access and historical reproducibility without re-parenting or silently falsifying assessment provenance.

## AT-OI-012 — Methodology Authority Input Contract

Define the exact methodology-owned inputs that must be supplied before production encoding: approved questions/answers, scoring/normalisation, ranking, ties/near-ties, genuine opposite pairs, possible-mask semantics, participant-facing terminology, report interpretation mappings, translations/rights and required methodology approval evidence.
