# 3. Upstream correction ledger before governed FP-003 finalisation

The following accepted correction contracts remain **mandatory upstream work** before a governed FP-003 Final Feature Pack Contract may rely on the affected semantics.

## AT-UPD-001 — Assessment-credit consumption authority

Correct Product/Roadmap wording so the applicable assessment right is consumed at the first durably accepted **scored** answer, not only after successful assessment/report delivery.

Post-start technical failure is recovery/remediation/refund territory and must not rewrite historical consumption as though use never occurred.

## AT-UPD-002 — Methodology-authority Domain alignment

Correct stale Domain wording that associates Four-Colour methodology authority with Venessa/Super Admin status.

Temperament owns approved methodology representation/lifecycle; substantive methodology authority comes from the governing IP agreement under current `DEC-309`, and technical/admin roles do not manufacture that authority.

## AT-UPD-004 — Result correction/invalidation/supersession Product semantics

Product Law must explicitly distinguish interpretation-only correction, proven computation-defect corrective successor, material methodology invalidation/remediation and participant regret.

Raw historical evidence remains immutable while retained; defective results may become superseded/ineligible for active use through an explicit governed relationship rather than mutation.

## AT-UPD-005 — FP-003 / OQ-017 assessment-warning routing

Roadmap must route the bounded DEC-056 assessment-expiry-warning need through FP-003 at the first protected use, without pulling the mature generic reminder system into FP-003.

Temperament owns expiry/applicability; Communications owns scheduled delivery/retry/evidence.

## AT-UPD-006 — Cross-domain identity reconciliation contract

Identity & Access owns canonical duplicate-account reconciliation. Temperament, Entitlements, Privacy and other owners reconcile their own truth idempotently without shared-write authority, answer merging, balance summation or destructive provenance loss.

## AT-UPD-007 — Ordinary annual reassessment timing

Product Law must define the ordinary one-retake-per-year rule as a rolling dual-clock restriction:

1. 12 calendar months from the latest qualifying valid digital completion; and
2. 12 calendar months from the latest ordinary reassessment first-answer/start commitment where one exists;

with valid entitlement required independently.

Premium entitlement availability does not override either guard.
