# 3. Corrected upstream / governance routing

## AT-UPD-001 — Assessment-right consumption semantics

**Classification:** `PRODUCT_AUTHORITY_GAP / ENTITLEMENT_SEAM_GAP`

Do not promote the prior universal first-scored-answer answer mechanically.

Product must explicitly adjudicate the authoritative consumption event by assessment-right type, including at least:

- ordinary standalone assessment right;
- included plan/bundle assessment credit;
- Premium annual reassessment right;
- technical/methodology remediation or replacement right where applicable.

Current authority contains competing signals: generic attempt-use language points to first answer, while included-credit Product/Roadmap wording points to successful digital delivery. Entitlements remains the commercial right owner; Temperament provides authoritative attempt/result/delivery facts according to the governed policy.

`AT-WD-001` remains a candidate for right types where Product adopts first-answer consumption; it is not universal law merely because it is coherent.

## AT-UPD-002 — Methodology-authority Domain wording

**Classification:** `DOMAIN_OWNERSHIP_CORRECTION REQUIRED`

The current Domain Map wording that ties methodology authority to Venessa/Super Admin status must be corrected to authority established by the governing IP agreement and scoped delegation under `DEC-309`.

Temperament continues to own the authoritative platform representation/lifecycle of approved methodology versions. Technical/admin roles do not create substantive methodology authority.

## AT-UPD-004 — Result correction / invalidation / supersession

**Classification:** `PRODUCT_AUTHORITY_GAP`

Current law governs raw-result immutability and Reviewed Interpretation, but Product must still decide the consequential semantics for:

- proven computation defects;
- corrective-result successors, if allowed;
- methodology invalidation/remediation;
- active-use eligibility after invalidation/supersession;
- current-profile consequences;
- report succession and participant notification.

`AT-WD-012` remains the parked candidate model. JIT must not assume all of it is already law.

## AT-UPD-005 — FP-003 / OQ-017 assessment-warning routing

**Classification:** `ROADMAP / DOCUMENTARY ROUTING CORRECTION`

The Roadmap should route the bounded DEC-056 assessment-expiry-warning need through FP-003 at the first protected use.

This must not pull the mature generic reminder platform into FP-003. Temperament owns expiry/applicability; Communications owns delivery scheduling/retries/quiet-hour handling/deduplication/evidence.

Exact 14d/7d/3d/24h cadence remains downstream configuration, not Product Law.

## AT-UPD-006 — Cross-domain identity reconciliation

**Classification:** `JIT + EXECUTABLE PROOF PRIMARY; PRODUCT ESCALATION ONLY IF NEEDED`

Current Architecture/Domain ownership already establishes that Identity owns authoritative identity reconciliation, Entitlements owns commercial rights, Temperament owns assessment truth and Privacy owns deletion/retention truth.

The normal unresolved work is therefore deterministic owner-mediated convergence, idempotency, active-attempt conflict handling and failure recovery in FP-003 / Identity / Entitlements JIT plus Phase-8 proof.

Promote new Product Law only if JIT exposes a participant-facing policy choice that current law genuinely cannot answer.

## AT-UPD-007 — Ordinary annual reassessment timing

**Classification:** `PRODUCT_AUTHORITY_GAP`

Product must explicitly choose the meaning of `one retake per year` and the annual use/attempt interval.

The `AT-WD-020` dual-clock model is the parked candidate because it prevents repeated abandoned retake attempts from bypassing the annual limit, but it is not current Product Law until governed.

JIT may not implement the dual-clock model merely because it is the working recommendation.

## AT-UPD-003 — Subscription completion promise

**Classification:** `FUTURE_ONLY`

No current Product promise guarantees one successfully completed initial digital assessment as a membership/subscription inclusion. Keep the parked semantics only as future design constraints unless Product deliberately creates that commercial promise.
