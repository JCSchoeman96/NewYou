# 1. Successor delta

## AT-WD-019 — Methodology Authority Input Contract

**Accepted:** `2026-10-08`

Production-intended Four-Colour assessment methodology may be encoded or published only from an identifiable, rights-backed, versioned methodology package approved by the methodology authority established by the governing IP agreement. The package must be semantically complete for every methodology behaviour enabled by that version and sufficient to reproduce the participant-facing assessment and deterministic raw result.

### AT-WD-019A — Rights and authority evidence precede production methodology encoding

The governed input package must establish the applicable rights basis and methodology-approval authority/delegation before proprietary production behaviour is encoded. Technical administrator or Super Admin power alone is not methodology authority.

Applicable rights/authority scope must cover the production behaviour actually enabled, including where relevant digital/commercial assessment, questions/answer structure, scoring, interpretation, tie/mask rules, report/derivative use, temperament-personalisation, translation, historical retention, economic/use and methodology-approval rights.

Book reproduction rights remain separate. The full book, EPUB, InDesign package or substantial copyrighted excerpts are not made public-repository inputs merely by this contract.

### AT-WD-019B — A startable methodology version requires complete deterministic scoring semantics

For every enabled launch behaviour, the approved methodology package must be sufficient to determine the governed outcome from the exact valid submitted answer set without engineering judgement.

Where applicable this includes:

- exact approved assessment questions and answer options;
- stable logical identities for scored items/options;
- scored/required-input designation;
- conditional-input requirements;
- question/answer mappings and weights;
- calculation/normalisation semantics;
- treatment of invalid/missing inputs;
- colour-score representation;
- classification/ranking semantics;
- tie/near-tie detection and resolution;
- tie-break question ordering/effects;
- unresolved ambiguity/equal-mixed behaviour; and
- any other enabled methodology-owned classification consequence.

Exact Ash Resources, database schemas and algorithm implementation remain downstream work.

### AT-WD-019C — Missing, ambiguous or contradictory methodology fails closed

Engineering, Product, Content, administrators or support staff may not invent a missing methodology rule or choose among contradictory governed inputs.

If approved-source artifacts disagree materially about scoring, classification, tie/mask behaviour, question meaning or other methodology truth, the affected methodology version is incomplete/not startable until methodology authority resolves the contradiction.

### AT-WD-019D — Tie and near-tie rules must terminate deterministically or preserve governed ambiguity

Where ties/near ties are possible, the methodology package must define the finite governed decision process: detection, required additional inputs, deterministic effects, termination and the conditions under which unresolved ambiguity/equal-mixed output remains legitimate.

Participant preference remains separate from raw scoring. Manual review remains exceptional and may not substitute for an undefined ordinary scoring algorithm.

### AT-WD-019E — Mask capability has an independent completeness gate

Mask/possible-mask behaviour may be enabled only when methodology authority has defined the method sufficiently, including as applicable:

- mask meaning;
- possible-mask triggers;
- genuine opposite-pair treatment;
- childhood/natural-self semantics;
- extrovert/introvert distinctions;
- task/relationship distinctions;
- required additional questions;
- scoring/classification consequences;
- legitimate unresolved ambiguity;
- methodology-review requirements; and
- participant-facing terminology/limitations.

Until complete, mask behaviour remains disabled rather than being inferred from opposite-pair scores or book prose.

### AT-WD-019F — Optional score-distance labels remain separately gated

Dominance-strength, balanced, close-result, mixed-distance or equivalent optional labels do not block an otherwise complete core assessment where Product Law permits those labels to remain disabled.

If enabled, their exact thresholds/rules require the separately governed methodology approval (including current `OQ-006` as applicable).

### AT-WD-019G — Afrikaans/English methodology variants require rights, language governance and meaning compatibility

Afrikaans and English assessment variants are governed counterparts of the same methodology version, not casual UI translations.

A participant-facing scored variant requires applicable translation/use rights, language review and sufficient methodology approval/confirmation that translated wording preserves the intended methodological meaning. Linguistic review alone cannot authorise methodology-sensitive wording.

Exact translation Resource design remains downstream `OQ-013`/Content/JIT work.

### AT-WD-019H — Report methodology basis is narrower than the full book

FP-003 must not treat the full book as an executable report specification.

Methodology authority supplies/approves the methodology-sensitive interpretation basis required for governed digital results/reports. Content & Media owns exact governed report copy/version/publication; Health/Clinical authority separately governs health/safety truth where report statements cross that boundary.

Report copy may express approved methodology but may not invent or silently change it.

### AT-WD-019I — Methodology, Product application, Content/translation and Health/Clinical authority remain distinct

Each governed rule must retain its correct authority class. Methodology authority controls assessment-method truth. Product authority may version low-risk behavioural application rules only where they do not redefine methodology. Content/translation publication controls participant-facing governed copy/variants within its authority. Health/Clinical authority controls health/safety truth.

Co-location in one file/table/admin screen does not transfer authority.

### AT-WD-019J — Publication is semantically atomic for enabled behaviour

A methodology version may become startable only when all required dependencies for its enabled production behaviour are complete, mutually consistent and approved under their respective authorities.

Questions may not be published as an active methodology version while required scoring/tie/language or other enabled methodology semantics remain unresolved. Optional disabled capabilities do not need to be completed merely for completeness.

Operator publication is distinct from methodology approval and does not elevate unapproved content.

### AT-WD-019K — Material methodology changes create explicit successors

Question meaning changes, scoring/weight changes, classification/ranking changes, tie-rule changes and mask-method changes require explicit methodology succession under methodology authority.

Non-methodology changes follow their own authority/versioning paths where they genuinely preserve methodology meaning, for example approved report prose improvements or low-risk Product presentation rules.

Started attempts remain pinned to their governed execution context under `AT-WD-011` unless explicit invalidation/remediation authority applies.

### AT-WD-019L — Published methodology retains auditable approval and compatibility provenance

For every published methodology version the platform must be able to establish the exact governed inputs/version set, rights basis, approving methodology authority/delegation scope, approval time/evidence, compatible language/content variants, enabled optional methodology capabilities and successor/retirement relationship.

The semantic requirement is unambiguous immutable approval provenance; database tables, signature technology, Git storage, workflow packages and other mechanisms remain downstream design choices.

# 3. Pressure-test additions — v0.15.0

## AT-PT-150 — Questions approved but scoring absent

**Scenario:** approved question text exists but scoring mappings/weights required for launch are not approved.

**Expected:** production methodology version is not startable; engineering may not infer scoring.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019B/C.

## AT-PT-151 — Core scoring exists but tie behaviour is undefined

**Scenario:** ordinary scores are defined but equal scores can occur and no governed tie rule exists.

**Expected:** version is incomplete/not startable for that behaviour; no arbitrary first/alphabetic/random winner.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019C/D.

## AT-PT-152 — Optional score-distance thresholds absent

**Scenario:** core launch methodology is complete but dominance/close/balanced labels are not approved.

**Expected:** core assessment may proceed with those optional labels disabled; they are not invented.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019F.

## AT-PT-153 — Mask rules absent

**Scenario:** approved core scoring exists but mask methodology is incomplete.

**Expected:** mask/possible-mask classification remains disabled; opposite pairs are not auto-labelled masks.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019E.

## AT-PT-154 — Super Admin uploads new weights

**Scenario:** technical administrator changes scoring weights without methodology-authority approval.

**Expected:** cannot publish/start the changed methodology solely by administrator role.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019A/I/J.

## AT-PT-155 — Methodology approval exists but digital commercial rights do not

**Scenario:** methodology authority approves content but governing rights instrument does not authorise the production digital/commercial assessment use.

**Expected:** production encoding/publication remains blocked until rights authority is established.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019A.

## AT-PT-156 — Translation right absent for Afrikaans variant

**Scenario:** English digital assessment rights exist but Afrikaans translation/use rights are not established.

**Expected:** Afrikaans production variant cannot be published merely because the text can be translated.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019A/G.

## AT-PT-157 — Afrikaans linguistically reviewed but methodology meaning unconfirmed

**Scenario:** translator approves fluency but methodology-sensitive question meaning has not been approved/confirmed.

**Expected:** variant is not startable as a governed assessment version.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019G/J.

## AT-PT-158 — English and Afrikaans scored item meanings materially diverge

**Scenario:** two language variants would elicit materially different methodological meaning.

**Expected:** fail closed; do not call them equivalent variants of the same startable methodology until authority resolves the divergence.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019G/C.

## AT-PT-159 — Two approved-source artifacts disagree on scoring

**Scenario:** one governed source maps Q12 to Red while another maps the same answer to Green.

**Expected:** no latest-file heuristic or engineering judgement; version remains invalid/incomplete until methodology authority resolves the contradiction.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019C.

## AT-PT-160 — Report author invents new temperament interpretation

**Scenario:** report copy introduces a methodology claim not present in the approved interpretation basis.

**Expected:** prohibited until appropriate methodology approval; Content publication authority cannot create methodology truth.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019H/I.

## AT-PT-161 — Product changes reminder/presentation style by temperament

**Scenario:** Product versions a low-risk behavioural delivery rule that does not alter assessment scoring/result meaning or safety.

**Expected:** may follow Product application authority without requiring a methodology successor, subject to DEC-309 boundary and other applicable authority.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019I/K.

## AT-PT-162 — Health reviewer rejects a health-pattern statement

**Scenario:** methodology/report material contains a health-pattern statement rejected by governing Health/Clinical authority.

**Expected:** methodology approval cannot override Health/Clinical truth; affected participant-facing report/application fails the applicable health review until corrected.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019H/I/J.

## AT-PT-163 — Book contains an extra rule not present in approved digital package

**Scenario:** staff discovers an interesting temperament rule in the book/EPUB that is absent from the rights-backed approved digital methodology package.

**Expected:** engineering does not encode it into production assessment logic merely because it appears in source material.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019A/C/H.

## AT-PT-164 — Methodology successor published while M1 attempt active

**Scenario:** participant started under M1; approved M2 later becomes active.

**Expected:** ordinary publication does not mutate the existing attempt; it remains pinned to M1 under `AT-WD-011`, unless M1 is explicitly invalidated through governed correction/remediation authority.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019K + AT-WD-011/012.

## AT-PT-165 — Delegation scope covers translation but not scoring

**Scenario:** delegate is authorised to approve translations but not scoring changes.

**Expected:** translation approval does not confer scoring authority; delegation scope is enforced.

**Result:** `PASS` under AT-WD-019A/G/I/L.
